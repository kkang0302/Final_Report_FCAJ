# Summary Report: "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week"

### Event Objectives

- Showcase and pitch AI Agent projects developed during the Agentic AI Build Week.
- Share architectural designs, implementation details, and business values of different AI systems built on AWS.
- Learn from peer projects and gain feedback from AWS Solution Architects and judges.

### Key Highlights

#### Team 1: KFC Ordering Agent (First Prize AWS Track)
- **Core Idea:** Conversational AI Agent for KFC ordering via Zalo/WhatsApp without downloading an app.
- **Key Takeaway:** Always include a verification step to reduce AI hallucination; design modular architecture (Channel Adapter, Agent Core, Memory, Tools) so it can be easily adapted to other brands.
- **Cost Awareness:** Included Bedrock API and AWS infra cost analysis, making the project production-ready.

#### Team Signal Scout: Strategic Intelligence Platform (Second Prize)
- **Core Idea:** Multi-agent platform to crawl competitor data, analyze strategy, and predict business ROI.
- **Key Takeaway:** "Technology cannot triumph over the Problem" — focus on the business pain points rather than bragging about AWS services.
- **MVP Mindset:** In hackathons, a working 20% MVP is much better than a perfect 100% scope that does not run.

#### Team BL: AI Solution Architect Assistant
- **Core Idea:** AI assistant helping SAs generate architecture diagrams, cost estimations, and IaC templates.
- **Key Takeaway:** AI should boost human productivity (reducing preparation time from 2 days to 30 minutes) rather than replace them; context (uploading corporate standards and security guidelines) is king.

### Key Takeaways & Lessons Learned

- **Problem First:** Always start from real-world user problems, not from the technology or AWS services.
- **Storytelling in Pitching:** A good presentation should start with a compelling story (e.g., case studies) to grab attention.
- **Business & Execution Mindset:** A simple, functional demo that solves a real business pain point is always preferred over an overly complex architecture.
- **Teamwork:** Value open debates but prioritize aligning on a single direction to execute effectively.

### Applying to Work

- Shift mindset to focus on identifying real business requirements before selecting technology.
- Design clean, modular architectures for AI agents with a verification phase for users.
- Apply MVP principles to deliver incremental value quickly and validate solutions early.
